About us

We broker dry cargo business across bulk, breakbulk and multipurpose segments, from coastal parcels to full cargoes. Our desk follows the market through a continuous flow of vessel positions and cargo orders received daily from owners, operators and charterers worldwide, built up over more than a decade in the trade.

The desk is supported by Laycan AI, our in-house assistant that reads the incoming flow, keeps positions and orders structured, and helps the broker put the right ship against the right cargo — while every fixture decision stays with a person.

What we do

Voyage chartering

Fixing dry bulk and breakbulk cargoes: firm orders worked against suitable open tonnage, offers and negotiations handled end to end.

Tonnage placement

Employment for open vessels: positions circulated to the right charterers and matched against live cargo orders in the desk's market window.

Market coverage

Daily reading of positions and orders across basins — who is open, what is moving, and where the market stands for a given size and trade.
